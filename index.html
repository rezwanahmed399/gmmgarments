END-TO-END WORKFLOW

The "Yarn to Shipment" Journey

Every single stitch carries our dedication. From spinning raw cotton to final steam pressing and packaging, explore our transparent 6-stage manufacturing line.

STEP 01
YARN & FIBER

1. Yarn Sourcing & Testing

Procuring premium combed cotton, organic GOTS fiber, and stretch blends with verified tensile strength and colorfastness.

  • OEKO-TEX Class 1 yarn testing
  • Ring spun & combed options
STEP 02
FABRIC WEAVING

2. Knitting & Eco-Dyeing

State-of-the-art circular knitting machines for single jersey, pique, interlock and sustainable water-saving dyeing plants.

  • Zero discharge ETP water purification
  • Custom Pantone color matching
STEP 03
AUTOMATED CAD

3. CAD Pattern & Cutting

Computerized CAD grading and automatic blade cutting systems maximize fabric yield and guarantee millimeter-accurate seam notches.

  • Gerber / Lectra precision cutting
  • Grainline & shrinkage pre-shrunk
STEP 04
JUKI & BROTHER

4. Precision Sewing & Lines

Multi-line automated sewing units equipped with Juki & Brother machinery. Specialized stitching for lockstitch, overlock, and flatlock.

  • Inline quality checkpoints every 10 machines
  • Clean, reinforced seams and bartacks
STEP 05
AQL 1.5 CHECK

5. Wash Lab & AQL Inspection

Eco-friendly enzyme washing, ozone rinse, laser fading for denim, followed by stringent AQL 1.5/2.5 4-point visual inspection.

  • Metal detection & needle scan on 100% units
  • Industrial tunnel steam finishing
STEP 06
LOGISTICS

6. Custom Packing & Logistics

Custom hang-tagging, barcode labels, individual polybags, and corrugated carton boxing ready for safe regional & doorstep delivery.

  • FSC certified packaging cartons
  • Doorstep container & bulk dispatch
